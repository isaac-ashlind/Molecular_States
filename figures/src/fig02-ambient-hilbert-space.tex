% Figure 2, ambient Hilbert space. A slice of C (delta = 0) is a baseline with a comb of fibers, one spin space over
% every configuration, and on each fiber the components f_xi and f_zeta of Psi(X) stand as bars. Three configurations
% stand on the slice. Below, on the same theta scale, are the component functions and the density |f_xi|^2 + |f_zeta|^2,
% so each sample stands above its own value.
% Declared in the caption, the state drawn is physical on the slice (rotation-invariant and exchange-antisymmetric,
% f_zeta = -f_xi), with f an illustrative Gaussian (figures/data). Red is oxygen, teal and gold the two components.
