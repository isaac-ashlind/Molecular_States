% Figure 2. Ambient Hilbert space. What the reader must see: one spin space over every configuration; a state
% assigns to each configuration a vector of that space (two components here); the norm is the integral of the
% squared length over C. Drawing: a slice of C as a baseline with a comb of fibres, three configurations standing
% on it, the two components as bars on each fibre; the component functions plotted beneath on the same theta scale.
% Colours: red is oxygen only; the two components f_xi and f_zeta are the dual pair teal and gold (free on this plate). Hatch unused.
% The plot shares the theta scale of the comb, so each sample stands above its value of |f_xi|^2+|f_zeta|^2; no marks repeat that.
% The state is physical on this slice: rotation-invariant and exchange-antisymmetric, so f_zeta = -f_xi (the proton
% singlet times f(theta), f an illustrative Gaussian, declared); samples and functions from figures/data.

% ------------------------------------------------ the comb: fibres over a slice of C
\begin{scope}[shift={(1.6,6.9)}]
  \draw[heavy] (-.2,0)--(9.6,0);
    \foreach \th in {80,90,...,180} {\pgfmathsetmacro{\x}{(\th-80)/100*9.4}\draw[fine] (\x,0)--(\x,2.6);}
  \node[sub,anchor=south] at (4.7,2.75) {$\spin$ over every $\X$, and on each fibre $\Psi(\X)=f_\xi\,\xi+f_\zeta\,\zeta$};

    \draw[line width=2.2pt,line cap=butt,draw=ColDualB] (\x+.09,1.3)--(\x+.09,{1.3+.9*\bv});}
