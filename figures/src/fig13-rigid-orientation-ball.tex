% Rigid methane (closing pages, unnumbered). Two columns that read as two figures (author).
% Left column: methane, CH4, the reference X0 in its body frame (mol3d-ch4-c2; the C2 axes x, y, z through the midpoints
% of opposite edges, thin, each split at the carbon by depth, z drawn longer so that its front half clears hydrogen 2),
% and beside it the orientation space SO(3) as the axis-angle ball (direction the axis, distance the angle, radius pi;
% the skin is the half-turns, each point its own antipode): the sphere and its three great circles teal; the twelve
% rotations of T as version points (the centre, the eight third-turns at the corners of the thin ink cube they span,
% the three half-turns on the skin drawn once each at their far side, faded); the cell of the centre as the gold
% octahedron dual to that cube, its vertices the quarter-turns (ink rings) on the cube's face centres; the lift of the
% third-turn loop as the one heavy arrow, passing behind the gold edge in front of it. The octahedron is the schematic
% exact in Rodrigues coordinates; in the ball the true edges bow outward (the caption says so).
% Right column: the covering in figure 11's grammar: the gold base patch SO(3)/T with the loop l_g, three of the twelve
% sheets r, r R_g, r R_g^2, the lift as figure 11's bow from the dot on sheet r to the dot on sheet r R_g, and one
% vertical dotted line of projection through every dot, hidden behind each sheet and re-emerging at its dot, running
% on past the top sheet: the stack goes on. The sheets are the cartoon of figure 11, not a computed surface.
% Both panels share one right-handed camera (make_data: CAM_BALL = CAM_MOL), so bond 1 points along the arrow.
% Suite rules: hidden contours dashed (back), projections dotted, version points \hatom; text under an object centred,
% lowercase phrases without full stops. Every coordinate from figures/data (make_data), the geometry recomputed in
% checks/verify_methane.py.
