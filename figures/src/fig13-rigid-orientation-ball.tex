% Rigid methane (closing pages, unnumbered): five panels in two rows, the cover's vocabulary.
% Row 1. Left, from the molecule camera (make_data: CAM_MOL, azimuth 346, elevation 64, chosen by scan for the
% clearest glyph with every C2 axis at least 26 degrees off the view): the reference X0 drawn with the suite's
% molecule primitive (mol3d-ch4-c2), the C2 axes x, y, z through the midpoints of opposite edges as thin lines behind
% it. Middle, the solid F for methane cut open as on the cover: the core is the ball SO(3) (teal), the wall the nine
% vibrations (white, its cut faces hatched); an octant is removed and the viewer looks in. "Stripped to its core"
% (figure 10's words) leads to the right panel: from the ball camera (CAM_BALL, azimuth 108, elevation 21, chosen by
% scan to sit 27 degrees from every symmetry axis of the cube so that nothing overlaps) the orientation space SO(3)
% in Rodrigues coordinates (direction the axis, distance tan(theta/2); the half-turns at infinity, each axis drawn
% once with an arrow toward them): the twelve symmetry rotations of T as red dots, the centre and the eight
% third-turns at (+-1,+-1,+-1), the vertices of a cube drawn with thin ink edges; one cell of the twelve, the exact
% octahedron |x|+|y|+|z| <= 1, in gold, with the six quarter-turns as ink rings at its vertices, which are the face
% centres of the cube; the lift of the third-turn loop as the one heavy arrow, from the centre through a face to the
% dot beyond. Every line is straight in these coordinates; nothing is approximated.
% Row 2. Left, under the solid: the covering in figure 11's grammar: a patch of SO(3)/T (the cell with its faces
% glued, figure 11's gold tint) with the loop l_g, the sheets r, r R_g, r R_g^2 of the twelve over it, the lift of
% l_g from one sheet to the next, one dotted projection (the convention of the cover). Right, under the ball: figure
% 12's rings of K on the sphere |J| for J = 2, the body axis z along the bond to hydrogen 1, each ring named by what
% the third-turn does to it, omega^K: A when 3 divides K, 1E or 2E otherwise.
% Line vocabulary (author, 2026-10-05): solid for what faces the viewer, dashed for what lies behind; dotted only for
% the projection. Colours: the sphere and the core teal (the orientation space takes over the core sheet's role), the
% cell gold (the chart patch, as in figures 10 and 11), the cube in thin ink, the dots red, the lift in ink; hatch is
% cut material. Every coordinate from figures/data (make_data), the geometry recomputed in checks/verify_methane.py.
