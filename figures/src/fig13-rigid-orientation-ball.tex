% Rigid methane (closing pages, unnumbered): three panels. Left, from the molecule camera (make_data: CAM_MOL, azimuth
% 346, elevation 64, chosen by scan for the clearest glyph with every C2 axis at least 26 degrees off the view): the
% reference X0 drawn with the suite's molecule primitive (mol3d-ch4-c2), the C2 axes x, y, z through the midpoints of
% opposite edges as thin lines behind it. Middle, from the ball camera (CAM_BALL, azimuth 108, elevation 21, chosen
% by scan to sit 27 degrees from every symmetry axis of the cube so that nothing overlaps): the orientation space
% SO(3) as the axis-angle ball (direction the axis, distance the angle, radius pi drawn 2 cm; the skin is the
% half-turns, each point its own antipode), its three great circles in the coordinate planes for depth, the twelve
% symmetry rotations of T as red dots: the centre, the eight third-turns at the vertices of a cube (drawn with thin
% edges, a tetrahedron through the bonds and one through the faces), the three half-turns on the skin at the crossings
% of the circles, each drawn once; one cell of the twelve as a wire octahedron with the six quarter-turns as ink rings
% at its vertices; the lift of the third-turn loop as a solid arrow from the centre through a face to the dot beyond.
% Right, the covering in figure 11's grammar: a patch of SO(3)/T with the loop l_g, the sheets r, r R_g, r R_g^2 of
% the twelve over it, the lift from one sheet to the next, one dotted projection (the convention of the cover).
% Line vocabulary of this plate (author, 2026-10-05): solid for what faces the viewer, dashed for what lies behind;
% dotted only for the projection. Colours untangle the nested surfaces (author): the sphere teal (the orientation
% space takes over the core sheet's role), the cell gold (the chart patch, as in figures 10 and 11; the base patch
% at right carries figure 11's gold tint), the cube in thin ink, the dots red, the lift in ink. Every coordinate
% from figures/data (make_data), the geometry recomputed in checks/verify_methane.py.
