% Rigid methane (closing pages, unnumbered): three panels from one camera (make_data: CAM_BALL, azimuth 108,
% elevation 21, chosen by scan to sit 27 degrees from every symmetry axis of the cube so that nothing overlaps).
% Left: the reference X0 in its body frame, the C2 axes x, y, z through the midpoints of opposite edges (fine lines),
% the hydrogens on the body diagonals with their digits.
% Middle: the orientation space SO(3) as the axis-angle ball (direction the axis, distance the angle, radius pi, on
% the page 2 cm; the skin is the half-turns, each point its own antipode): the twelve symmetry rotations of T as red
% dots (the centre; eight third-turns, a tetrahedron through the bonds and one through the faces; three half-turns
% on the skin, each drawn once), one cell of the twelve as a wire octahedron (hidden edges dotted, the only dotted
% lines on the plate) with the six quarter-turns as ink rings at its vertices, and the lift of the third-turn loop:
% a solid arrow from the centre through a face to the dot beyond.
% Right: the covering in figure 11's grammar: a patch of the quotient SO(3)/T (one cell with its faces glued) with the
% loop l_g, and over it the sheets r, r R_g, r R_g^2 of the twelve (one per rotation of T); the lift of l_g runs from
% the dot on sheet r to the dot on sheet r R_g; one dotted projection from the base point to its sheet, the
% convention of the cover and of figure 11.
% Ink, white, the red dots; no other colour. Every coordinate from figures/data/numbers.tex (make_data), the
% geometry recomputed in checks/verify_methane.py.
