% Figure 11. Covering spaces and monodromy. What the reader must see: over a free domain V the twelve sheets
% group in six version pairs; a loop downstairs lifts to a path that climbs from one sheet to another, and
% continuation multiplies the sheet label on the right, so t-then-b and b-then-t end on different sheets.
% Drawing: twelve oblique plates in six pairs over the tinted base V with the two loops through [X] and the
% two lifted paths climbing to different plates (the loop figure after Harter (1993) fig. 5.4.2 in spirit:
% a closed path downstairs whose lift does not close); the six t,b-sheets as a graph for the bookkeeping;
% X and bX as two solid configurations in a magnifier. Colours: blue = t, red dashed = b, gold = endpoints.
% Hatch unused. The relations bt = t^2 b, btb = t^-1, the residual 0.76 A and the free-action margin are computed.
% Chosen from studies/alternatives/alt-fig04-08-11 (stack of plates as the drawing, graph as bookkeeping).
