% Figure 11. Covering spaces and monodromy. What the reader must see: over a patch of the quotient F/G the sheets group in
% version pairs {gX, gbX}; a loop downstairs lifts to a path from one sheet to another; t then b and b then t end on
% different sheets. Drawing: the pairs side by side over V, the X-side column at the left and the bX-side at the
% right, each headed by its configuration; t climbs a column (solid), b hops across (dotted, the starred generator);
% t then b ends one level up on the right, b then t two levels up on the right (gold rings); b hops straight across; the six sheets as a
% graph at the right for the bookkeeping, where t turns the outer triangle one way and the inner the other.
% The loop figure after Harter (1993) fig. 5.4.2 in spirit (a closed path downstairs whose lift does not close).
% Colours: red = the points over [X] (versions), gold = the endpoints, blue = nitrogen; ink elsewhere. Hatch unused.
% The u-half of the twelve sheets is alike and left to the caption. The relations bt = t^2 b, btb = t^-1 and the
% best-fit residual between X and bX are computed (checks/).

  \sheet{0}{0}{heavy}{ColRetained!50}

  \foreach \k/\ga/\gb/\pl in {0/{\X}/{b\X}/H,1/{t\X}/{tb\X}/tH,2/{t^2\X}/{t^2b\X}/t^2H} {

    \node[lab,anchor=east] at (-4.3,{\y+.45}) {$\pl$};}
  \draw[projection,shorten <=2.3pt,shorten >=2.3pt] (bx)--(pa0); \draw[projection,shorten <=2.3pt,shorten >=2.3pt] (bx)--(pb0);   % the projections, dotted as on the cover
