% Figure 10. Position representation. What the reader must see: the solid F of figure 6 stripped of its cladding is the
% core sheet, the reference family X0(a), an open cylinder in a = (tau, iota); cut at the seam tau = +-pi and
% unrolled it is the chart [-pi, pi] x [-iota0, iota0] with the six H-cells and the reference cell U, the same six
% version points and the same two paths; over each point a of the chart sit every normal displacement q and every
% orientation r, which is the reconstruction x = (r, a, q) -> R(r)(X0(a) + sum q e(a)), drawn as the shape, the shape
% displaced along the normal frame (its undisplaced ghost beneath) and the result rotated; the two normal directions
% e_-(a) (the amino twist) and e_+(a) (the C-N stretch) are drawn on the molecule from the side. Colours: red =
% version points, gold (pale) = the reference cell, teal = the sheet; ink elsewhere, t solid, tu dashed. Hatch = cut
% material (the solid's cut faces). Cells, normal directions and the displaced and rotated copies computed
% (compute/, checks/); the displacement amplitude and the rotation are illustrative (declared).
