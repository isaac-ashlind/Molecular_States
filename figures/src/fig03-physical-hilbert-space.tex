% Figure 3. Physical Hilbert space. What the reader must see: the spins stay with the places and only the digits
% move under a relabelling, so the value at the relabelled configuration is the permuted value up to the statistics
% sign; inversion sends every column through the origin and costs the parity sign instead. Drawing: three
% computed configurations X, sigma X and -X with spin arrows beside the nuclei and digits inside the hydrogens,
% the two operations as arrows carrying their sign. Colours: oxygen red only; gold unused. Hatch unused.
% Chosen from studies/alternatives/alt-fig03 (approach A for exchange; the parity panel in the same manner,
% the handedness glyph dropped: water is planar and -X is also a rotated copy, stated on the plate).
