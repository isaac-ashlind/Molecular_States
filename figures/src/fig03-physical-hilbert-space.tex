% Figure 3. Physical Hilbert space. What the reader must see: a relabelling moves the digits and leaves the spins
% with the places, so the value at the relabelled configuration is the permuted spin value times the statistics
% sign; inversion sends every column through the origin and costs the parity sign instead. Drawing: the top row is
% the three configurations X, sigma X = X P_sigma and -X P_sigma = E* sigma X with spin arrows beside the nuclei
% (up teal, down gold) and digits inside the hydrogens; the bottom row is the spin value itself, two coloured arrows
% in a bracket in slot order, permuted under sigma and carrying the sign, then the parity sign under E*.
% Colours: oxygen red; the dual pair teal (spin up) and gold (spin down). Hatch unused. Planar water: -X P_sigma is
% also a rotated copy (caption).
