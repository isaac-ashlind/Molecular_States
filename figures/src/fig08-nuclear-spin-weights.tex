% Figure 8. Nuclear spin weights. What the reader must see: the 32-dimensional proton spin space splits under G6
% into copies of A1, A2 and E; a spatial level of species Gamma pairs with the spin species whose product with
% Gamma contains the parity character, and its weight is the number of copies of that partner; even and odd parity
% swap the A1 and A2 partners. Drawing: a correlation diagram in the manner of Harter (1993) fig. 4.2.3, spatial
% species as level rules at left, spin species at right as partitioned bars whose length is the copy count
% (32 units in all), solid connections for even parity, dashed for odd. Colours: bars tinted by species; ink
% elsewhere; gold unused. Hatch unused. All counts from the character computation (checks/verify.g).
% Chosen from studies/alternatives/alt-fig04-08-11 (correlation diagram merged with the partitioned bar).
