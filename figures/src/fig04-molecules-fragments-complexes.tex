% Figure 4. Molecules, fragments, complexes. What the reader must see: a grouping is a choice of boundaries drawn
% on one configuration, not a change of positions; for KRb + KRb the three groupings carry three channel groups
% that sit inside S, meet in <E*>, and any two generate S; group inclusion says nothing about accessibility.

% the lattice, the orders, the core and the generation facts are computed (compute/, checks/).
