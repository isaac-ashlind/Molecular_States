% Figure 4, molecules, fragments and complexes. At the top one set of four nuclei is grouped three ways along the
% reaction, KRb + KRb, the complex K2Rb2 and K2 + Rb2, each grouping with its permutation group by generators. The two
% product channels are two strands, gold for potassium and teal for rubidium, parallel into the complex and apart out
% of it. Below, the same strands run through the lattice of the sixteen subgroups of S, up from G_in to S and down to
% G_K and G_Rb. The other subgroups are faint.

\path[use as bounding box] (0,0) rectangle (15,11.0);
