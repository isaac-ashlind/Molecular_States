# Round 5: rigid-recovery mockups (rejected)

Three mockups of the methane recovery in the paper's grammar, drawn before
the logical thread was settled. All three were rejected by the author
("looks trashy"): each packs three rows of borrowed material (figure 10
solid, figure 11 sheets, figure 7 mode discs, figure 8 bundles, figure 12
rings) with no single picture to prove.

- `recovery-A.tex`: solid -> point -> sheets over SO(3)/T; twelve-site modes; spin bundles.
- `recovery-B.tex`: methane with the third-turn; the K rings with the seam rule; the J ladder.
- `recovery-C.tex`: the twelve elements of T as four triangles of relabellings.
- `modes12.tex`: the twelve-site modes of C[T] = A + 1E + 2E + 3T, reusable.

Settled thread for the next attempt: for rigid methane H = G = Td(M), order 24,
so there is one version (G/H) and two labelled copies (S/H). The shape chart
collapses to a point; the orientation space SO(3), drawn as the axis-angle ball,
takes over the role of the chart, tiled by twelve octahedral cells centred on
the twelve rotations of T (centre, two tetrahedra of third-turns, three
half-turns on the skin). Versions become orientations.
