## Aesthetic identity (locked unless the author amends it)
1. Two ink weights, no grey linework: heavy 1.0 pt for silhouettes, principal arrows and section edges;
   fine 0.4 pt for construction, leaders, hatch and hidden lines (dashed). Labels 9 pt serif math in ink.
2. Shading is bold and solid: a black crescent on the shadow side of spheres; a solid black band bent
   around tubes; regular 45-degree hatch for walls, ground and cut faces. No engraving, no stipple, no
   gradients.
3. Flat gouache under ink: atoms are flat colour discs with ink contour and black crescent
   (H white, C ink, N #397BA8, O #D56860, K #91A653, Rb #AD8BC0); gold #E0B84F only for marked points
   and highlighted regions. Colour otherwise only on paths. One meaning per colour per figure.
4. 3D is constructed: oblique or perspective solids with hidden lines dashed; ghost copies (dashed,
   uncoloured) for rotated or relabelled versions; cut-aways to show thickness; bonds as rods (double line).
5. One idea per panel, at most three panels per figure, whitespace kept; annotations are short labels,
   the author's captions carry the words.
