# Storyline of the plates, part by part

The sections now follow the figure flow (docs/manuscript-reorganized.tex). Each plate hands one object to the next.

Part I, Configurations and States.
1. A configuration is a centred matrix: the water family over its two shape coordinates, and what the family leaves
   out, rotation on the left and permutation or inversion on the right (the commuting square). Hands over: X, RX, XP.
2. One spin space over every configuration: the comb of fibres on a slice, the norm as an integral; the drawn state
   is physical (the proton singlet on that slice). Hands over: Psi(X) as components.
3. The two conditions that cut the physical space: spins stay with the places while the digits move (exchange sign),
   every column through the origin (parity sign). Hands over: relabelling as an operation on configurations.

Part II, Fragments and Feasible Regimes.
4. Molecules and complexes through one reaction: KRb + KRb as three groupings of four nuclei and as a path in the
   subgroup lattice of S. Hands over: groupings are boundaries; groups sit in a lattice.
5. Methylamine as two fragments (the transition), the reference X0 and its half-turn partner bX0, the whole interval
   [H, S] carved to the bond interval [H, B] with the chain in blue, the six versions as moved digits. Hands over:
   H, G, the versions, the two generators t and tu.
6. The feasible solid: the family thickened into a short tube, cut open, the two generator paths and the version
   points on its band, the Newman projections along the paths, twenty images in C. Hands over: the band and its
   points, which every later plate reuses.

Part III, Symmetry and Localization.
7. The three version states as sites: the coset triangle and the mode pictures A1 and E.
8. The spin space as bars, the pairing with the spatial species by parity (correlation diagram).
9. Packets standing on the torsion circle, the overlap and the shares.

Part IV, Representations and Global Structure.
10. The band of figure 6 cut at the seam and unrolled into the chart with the six cells; the slice at q is the cut
    face of figure 6.
11. The sheets over the quotient patch with the two lifts ending apart; X and bX as two configurations over one point.
12. The K ladder on the sphere, the sheared lattice of torsional labels, the periodic modes on the torsion circle
    sorted into species.

Consistent elements: blue = t, red dashed = tu or b, gold = version points and packets, hatch = removed material,
the torsion circle (9, 12), the band and its six points (6, 10, guide), the carving idiom in the lattices (4, 5).
